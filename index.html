Support

Need help?

We're here to help. Check the FAQs below or reach out directly — we respond within 48 hours.

How do I add subscriptions to Recurrly?

You can add subscriptions two ways: (1) Tap the "+" button and enter details manually, or (2) Upload a PDF bank statement and our AI will automatically detect all subscriptions for you.

How does the AI PDF scanner work?

When you upload a PDF, we securely send it to OpenAI's GPT-4 to extract subscription names, prices, and billing frequencies. The data is then displayed for you to review and add. PDFs are not stored after processing.

How do I cancel a subscription I'm tracking?

Recurrly tracks your subscriptions but cancellation happens directly with the service provider. Tap any subscription to see details and instructions for canceling that specific service.

Is my data private and secure?

Yes. All data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Authentication is handled by Clerk (SOC 2 compliant) and data is stored on Supabase. We never sell your data and do not share it with advertisers.

How do I delete my account?

Email us at elavignesh@gmail.com from your account email and we will delete your account and all associated data within 30 days. An in-app deletion option is coming in a future update.

Why isn't a subscription showing up after PDF scan?

The AI may occasionally miss transactions that aren't clearly labeled as subscriptions. You can always add missing ones manually using the "+" button.

Still need help?

Email us and we'll get back to you within 48 hours.
